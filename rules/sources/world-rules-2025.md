## Scope

第二章 三 (terms: card set, level cards, 逢人配, ten combination types, lines 87–127) and 第三章 rank/compare clauses (lines 175–193). Its combination list and comparison clauses repeat JS17 almost word for word, and add one sentence JS17 lacks: "级牌在搭配成三连对、二连三、顺子、同花顺时，作原牌点" (level cards keep their natural rank inside sequences).
